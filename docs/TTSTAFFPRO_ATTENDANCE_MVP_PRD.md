# TTStaffPro Presence Attendance — MVP Product Requirements Document

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Product | TTStaffPro Presence Attendance |
| Version | 1.0 (MVP) |
| Date | 2026-08-26 |
| Platforms | Android first; Flutter |
| Primary users | Students, employees, lecturers/facilitators, managers, attendance administrators |
| Product decision | Attendance identity is established by authenticated account + signed session credential. Camera use, if enabled, is **face-presence only** and never identifies or matches a person. |

## 1. Executive summary

TTStaffPro Presence Attendance is a focused attendance product made of two Android applications:

1. **TTStaffPro Attend** — installed on each student or employee's phone. The user signs in, scans a time-limited attendance QR code, and receives an immediate attendance result.
2. **TTStaffPro Kiosk** — installed on a shared classroom, gate, or workplace Android device. A lecturer, facilitator, or manager starts an attendance session and displays a rotating QR code. It also shows live attendance counts and device health.

The product makes check-in quick, works during intermittent connectivity, prevents simple replay/share abuse, and synchronizes records to the TTStaffPro backend automatically.

This MVP deliberately does **not** use face recognition, face matching, face enrollment, face templates, or automatic identity from a camera. Those are not part of the product or its data model. An optional on-device camera step can only confirm that a face is currently in the camera frame; it is not an identity signal, does not store images, and does not upload frames.

## 2. Problem statement

Current attendance collection is slow, vulnerable to manual mistakes, and difficult to reconcile when connectivity fails. Staff and students need a low-friction way to record attendance at a specific class, shift, or event, while administrators need a reliable audit trail and a way to correct legitimate errors.

The MVP solves this by tying every attendance record to:

- an authenticated TTStaffPro account;
- an active, server-authorized class/shift/event session;
- a short-lived, signed QR challenge; and
- an auditable client event with an idempotency key.

## 3. Goals and success measures

### Goals

- Let a verified account check in or check out in under 10 seconds.
- Support classes, shifts, meetings, audits, and events using the same session model.
- Keep a valid local queue when internet connectivity drops and sync automatically when it returns.
- Give a session owner a live, understandable count of checked-in, pending-sync, duplicate, and rejected attempts.
- Make every attendance change traceable and correctable by authorized staff.
- Avoid retaining or processing biometric identity data.

### MVP success measures

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Median successful check-in time | <= 10 seconds |
| Successful online check-ins | >= 98% excluding invalid credentials/session expiry |
| Automatic sync of queued records | >= 99% within 15 minutes of connectivity returning |
| Duplicate attendance records | < 0.1% of attempts |
| QR replay attempts accepted | 0 |
| Camera frames, face templates, or face images retained by the product | 0 |
| Attendance corrections with an audit reason | 100% |

## 4. Product boundaries and privacy decision

### Included

- Account-based attendance for students and employees.
- Dynamic QR sessions generated by a kiosk/facilitator device.
- Optional on-device, non-identifying face-presence check before submitting a check-in.
- Optional location verification when an organization enables it and the user grants permission.
- Offline queue, background retry, idempotent backend synchronization, and manual retry.
- Attendance history, session roster, correction workflow, exports, and audit logs.
- Kiosk registration, branch/classroom assignment, heartbeat, and remote revocation.

### Explicitly excluded

- Face recognition or matching a camera image to a student/employee identity.
- Face enrollment, biometric templates/embeddings, face-profile packages, or matching thresholds.
- Storing, uploading, or retaining camera frames, selfies, snapshots, or biometric data.
- Automatic discipline, payroll, academic, or employment decisions based solely on attendance events.
- Continuous background tracking.

### Privacy principles

- The account/session credential, not a face, establishes the identity on an attendance record.
- The optional camera check runs locally. It returns only a transient boolean (`facePresent`) to the app and is discarded immediately after use.
- Camera access is optional per organization/session policy; users must have a QR/PIN fallback when it is not available.
- Location is off by default and must be disclosed before use. The product records a location verification result, not a continuous location trail.
- Attendance data has a configurable retention period. Audit events have a separate, documented retention period.
- Users can see their own attendance record and request correction through the established organization process.

## 6. Apps and scope

### 6.1 App A — TTStaffPro Attend (individual Android app)

**Purpose:** let a student or employee record their own attendance for a session.

Core screens:

1. Sign in / organization selection.
2. Home: next session, today's attendance status, **Scan to check in/out**, and pending-sync count.
3. QR scanner with permission education and manual-session-code fallback.
4. Optional local face-presence check screen (only when policy requires it).
5. Check-in result: accepted, already checked in, too early/late, not eligible, session closed, or pending sync.
6. Attendance history and detail screen.
7. Pending sync and retry screen.
8. Profile, privacy, permissions, and support.

### 6.2 App B — TTStaffPro Kiosk (shared Android app)

**Purpose:** create a trusted, visible attendance session in a classroom, workplace, or event location.

Core screens:

1. Device registration and administrator approval state.
2. Kiosk sign-in or managed-device launch.
3. Select session template / class / shift / event.
4. Start session: show a rotating QR code, session name, start/end time, and optional room/location state.
5. Live session dashboard: total expected, accepted, late, duplicate, pending sync, and current QR expiry countdown.
6. Session roster: names and attendance state only for staff with permission.
7. End session confirmation and summary.
8. Device health: connection, battery, storage, app version, last server contact, and sync queue count.

### 6.3 Admin capability (MVP extension, not a third mobile app)

The existing TTStaffPro admin portal should host the first administrative functions. This avoids duplicating access control and reporting in a third app during the MVP.

Required admin functions:

- Session templates and enrollment/roster import.
- Kiosk device approval, branch/classroom assignment, and revocation.
- Attendance record search, export, and correction workflow.
- Policy configuration: session timing, grace periods, camera-presence optional/required/off, location verification optional/required/off, QR rotation interval, and retention periods.
- Audit-log export.

## 7. Primary user journeys

### 7.1 Standard online check-in

```text
Facilitator starts session on Kiosk
  -> Kiosk obtains signed dynamic QR token from backend
  -> Attendee opens Attend and scans QR
  -> Attend validates format and checks session eligibility
  -> (optional) local face-presence check passes; no image is saved
  -> Attend posts an idempotent attendance event
  -> Backend verifies user, session, token, timing, and duplicate rules
  -> Attend shows accepted / check-in time
  -> Kiosk live count updates
```

### 7.2 Offline attendee check-in

```text
Attendee scans a currently valid QR while network is unavailable
  -> Attend records a signed local event with UUID and local timestamp
  -> Attend clearly shows “Recorded on device — pending sync”
  -> Connectivity returns
  -> Background sync sends the event once using the UUID idempotency key
  -> Backend accepts, rejects, or flags the event for review
  -> Attend and Kiosk refresh their status
```

**MVP policy:** offline check-in is allowed only if the QR contains a server-signed session token that has not expired under the device's last trusted time. If the device clock is unreliable or the QR has expired, the app must show a manual/facilitator fallback rather than inventing a time.

### 7.3 Correction request

```text
Attendee opens Attendance History
  -> selects an incorrect/missing record
  -> enters a reason and optional allowed attachment
  -> manager/admin reviews the request
  -> approved change creates an immutable audit event
  -> attendee receives the final result
```

## 8. Functional requirements

### 8.1 Identity and access

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AUTH-01 | The Attend app shall require authenticated TTStaffPro account access before a check-in can be submitted. | Must |
| AUTH-02 | The backend shall derive the attendee identity from the access token, never from a camera image or client-supplied person ID. | Must |
| AUTH-03 | The Kiosk app shall support device registration, approval, branch/classroom assignment, heartbeat, and revocation. | Must |
| AUTH-04 | Kiosk session controls shall require a facilitator role or managed-device entitlement. | Must |
| AUTH-05 | Tokens must be stored in platform-secure storage and cleared on logout/revocation. | Must |

### 8.2 Session and QR flow

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SES-01 | A facilitator can start a session from an assigned template/class/shift/event. | Must |
| SES-02 | A session has a unique ID, organization, roster scope, valid window, timezone, venue/branch, policy snapshot, and state. | Must |
| SES-03 | Kiosk shows a rotating QR credential with a default 30-second validity period. | Must |
| SES-04 | Each QR credential includes a signed session ID, nonce, expiry, issuer, and version. It must not contain student/employee details. | Must |
| SES-05 | The backend rejects expired, malformed, revoked, cross-organization, and replayed credentials. | Must |
| SES-06 | The Attend app offers a manual short code only when allowed by session policy; the code must be rate-limited and short-lived. | Should |
| SES-07 | A session supports check-in, check-out, and session-specific attendance types. | Must |

### 8.3 Attendance event processing

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ATT-01 | Every submission has a client-generated UUID idempotency key. Re-sending the same key must return the original result. | Must |
| ATT-02 | The backend validates account eligibility, enrollment/roster, session state, time window, QR signature, duplicate rules, and any enabled policy checks. | Must |
| ATT-03 | The result must distinguish accepted, duplicate, pending review, late, ineligible, expired, and rejected outcomes. | Must |
| ATT-04 | Check-in and check-out actions must be evaluated independently and retain their own timestamps. | Must |
| ATT-05 | A facilitator cannot silently edit a submitted event. Any correction creates a linked audit record with actor, reason, time, old value, and new value. | Must |
| ATT-06 | Server time is authoritative for online records. The client timestamp is retained only for audit and offline sequencing. | Must |

### 8.4 Optional camera-presence step

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAM-01 | Session policy may set camera presence to `off`, `optional`, or `required`. Default is `off`. | Must |
| CAM-02 | If enabled, the app uses on-device face detection only to determine whether one face is visible at the moment of submission. | Must |
| CAM-03 | The app must not compare the detected face to any reference image, profile, template, embedding, or database. | Must |
| CAM-04 | The app must not capture, upload, cache, log, or retain camera frames or face landmarks. | Must |
| CAM-05 | If camera permission is unavailable or detection fails, users must receive a documented QR/PIN/facilitator fallback; they must not be automatically marked absent. | Must |

### 8.5 Optional location and device checks

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LOC-01 | Location verification is disabled by default and can be enabled per organization/session. | Must |
| LOC-02 | When enabled, the backend evaluates a one-time location verification assertion against the permitted venue/geofence. | Should |
| LOC-03 | The product stores only the verification result and minimum audit data required by the configured policy; it does not track users continuously. | Must |
| DEV-01 | Kiosk reports heartbeat, network state, battery, storage state, app version, and pending-sync count. | Must |
| DEV-02 | A revoked or unassigned kiosk cannot create new sessions. | Must |

### 8.6 Offline and synchronization

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OFF-01 | Attend and Kiosk persist unsent events locally in an encrypted queue. | Must |
| OFF-02 | Each queued event stores the idempotency UUID, session ID, action, local timestamp, signed QR credential hash, retry state, and minimal policy evidence. | Must |
| OFF-03 | The queue automatically retries with exponential backoff when a connection is available and also provides a user-triggered retry. | Must |
| OFF-04 | Sync batches are limited, resumable, and report individual item outcomes. | Must |
| OFF-05 | An event rejected after sync remains visible with an explanation and correction path; it is never silently discarded. | Must |
| OFF-06 | Users can see a clear pending-sync badge and the last successful synchronization time. | Must |

### 8.7 Reporting and correction

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| REP-01 | The admin portal supports filtering records by organization, branch, class/shift, date, attendee, session, result, and sync state. | Must |
| REP-02 | Authorized staff can export attendance in CSV/XLSX using policy-controlled columns. | Should |
| REP-03 | Attendees can submit correction requests for their own records; managers/admins can decide them with a reason. | Must |
| REP-04 | Audit reports include kiosk device ID, app version, actor, source, session, idempotency UUID, timestamps, policy outcome, and correction chain. | Must |

## 9. Core data model

No table or API field in this MVP stores a face image, biometric template, face embedding, confidence score, or matching threshold.

### `attendance_session`

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `id` | Server-generated session UUID. |
| `organization_id`, `branch_id`, `venue_id` | Scope and authorization. |
| `template_id`, `title`, `session_type` | Class, shift, event, meeting, etc. |
| `starts_at`, `ends_at`, `timezone`, `grace_minutes` | Session timing. |
| `roster_scope` | Course/class/team/department or explicit roster. |
| `policy_snapshot` | QR, camera-presence, location, and correction policies at session start. |
| `state` | Draft, active, ended, cancelled. |
| `kiosk_device_id`, `facilitator_id` | Accountability. |

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `id`, `idempotency_uuid` | Stable server event ID and client retry key. |
| `organization_id`, `session_id`, `attendee_id` | Server-derived attendee identity. |
| `event_type` | Check-in, check-out, correction, reversal. |
| `result` | Accepted, duplicate, late, pending_review, rejected. |

| `device_id`, `app_version` | Diagnostics/audit. |
| `camera_presence_result` | Optional boolean only; no associated image or biometric record. |
| `location_verification_result` | Optional status/minimum audit result, per policy. |

### `sync_queue_item` (device-local)

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `idempotency_uuid` | Deduplication key. |
| `payload_version`, `encrypted_payload` | Minimal event payload; encrypted at rest. |
| `created_at`, `last_attempt_at`, `attempt_count` | Retry behavior. |
| `state` | Pending, syncing, synced, failed, needs_review. |
| `last_error_code` | Supportable failure state. |

## 10. Backend API contract (MVP)

The exact route names can follow existing TTStaffPro conventions. The contract below is the required behavior.

| Endpoint | Method | Caller | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/attendance-sessions` | POST | Kiosk/admin | Start a session; return session metadata and first QR credential. |
| `/attendance-sessions/{id}/credential` | POST | Kiosk | Mint the next short-lived signed QR credential. |
| `/attendance-sessions/{id}` | GET | Attend/Kiosk/admin | Retrieve policy-safe session status. |
| `/attendance-events` | POST | Attend/Kiosk | Submit one online attendance event with `Idempotency-Key`. |
| `/attendance-events/sync-batch` | POST | Attend/Kiosk | Submit queued events; return one result per UUID. |
| `/attendance-events/{uuid}` | GET | Attend/Kiosk | Resolve ambiguous result after a timeout. |
| `/attendance-sessions/{id}/roster` | GET | Kiosk/admin | Authorized live roster/counts. |
| `/attendance-corrections` | POST | Attend | Submit a correction request. |
| `/attendance-corrections/{id}/decision` | POST | Admin | Approve/reject with a reason. |
| `/attendance-kiosks/register` | POST | Kiosk | Register device and receive a scoped device token. |
| `/attendance-kiosks/heartbeat` | POST | Kiosk | Send device health and queue status. |
| `/attendance-kiosks/{id}/revoke` | POST | Admin | Block kiosk from starting sessions. |

### Example attendance event request

```json
{
  "idempotencyUuid": "0c754d36-6987-4d24-a64e-370e6e1dc002",
  "sessionId": "b93442e3-8954-4d80-9d8c-03ae0531b786",
  "eventType": "check_in",
  "qrCredential": "signed-short-lived-token",
  "clientOccurredAt": "2026-08-26T09:01:42+05:30",

  "cameraPresence": { "performed": true, "facePresent": true },
```

## 11. Business rules

1. A user can only check in to an active session for which their account is eligible.
2. The same account cannot produce two accepted check-ins for the same session unless an administrator explicitly reverses or corrects the first record.
3. A QR credential expires quickly and is valid only for its organization, session, and intended action.
4. A QR credential is not a reusable attendance ticket; backend nonce/replay controls make it single-use or bounded-use according to policy.
5. The server is the source of truth for online timing, session state, and final result.
6. Offline records are provisional until synchronized. The user must see that state clearly.
7. A rejected offline record stays visible with a reason and correction path.
8. Camera presence is never used to identify the user and is never the sole basis for an attendance decision.
9. If an optional verification mechanism fails technically, the product presents the organization-approved manual fallback; it does not infer absence or misconduct.
10. Corrections and exports are permissioned and always audit logged.

### Flutter implementation recommendation

Use Flutter for both Android apps because this workspace already contains a Flutter application and the relevant building blocks:

- `mobile_scanner` for QR scanning;
- `qr_flutter` for kiosk credential display;
- `camera` and `google_mlkit_face_detection` only for optional local face presence;
- `dio` for API communication;
- `hive` / `hive_flutter` for the encrypted local queue (with app-managed encryption key stored using secure storage);
- existing TTStaffPro authentication, attendance, location, and device utilities where compatible.

Do **not** wire either app to the current `face-attendance` profile, recognition, snapshot, profile-package, or sync-batch endpoints. Those endpoints and their face-related models should remain isolated from this MVP. Create a new neutral attendance-session API namespace rather than relabeling biometric routes.

## 13. Security, reliability, and compliance requirements

### Security

- TLS for every request; use certificate pinning only after operational certificate rotation is designed.
- Short-lived access tokens with refresh/revocation behavior aligned to existing TTStaffPro authentication.
- Android Keystore-backed secret storage for tokens and queue encryption key.
- Server-signed QR credentials using a managed signing key and key ID for rotation.
- Idempotency keys and backend replay protection on every attendance submission.
- Role-based access control at API level; never rely solely on hidden UI controls.
- Rate limit QR verification, manual-code entry, correction requests, and kiosk registration.
- Do not include raw QR secrets, authorization tokens, or personally identifying information in logs/crash reports.

### Reliability

- Queue events transactionally before reporting “pending sync” to the user.
- Retry only safe idempotent requests; resolve timed-out submissions by UUID before retrying.
- Detect and communicate device clock drift. Do not accept local time as server truth.
- Support limited offline queue capacity with a user-visible warning before it fills.
- Kiosk must gracefully recover after app restart and resume the active session only after server validation.
- Use server status events/polling for live kiosk counts; the initial MVP can use polling before introducing WebSockets.

### Accessibility and localization

- Support screen readers, large fonts, high contrast, haptic confirmation, and non-color-only status indicators.
- Provide English plus organization-required languages; all result/error codes need localized, actionable text.
- Do not make camera checks the only interaction path; QR/PIN/manual-facilitator fallbacks are required.

## 14. Error handling and user messages

| Situation | Attend app behavior | Kiosk/admin behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QR expired | Say “This code expired. Ask the facilitator to keep the QR screen open.” Offer rescan. | Automatically refresh QR; show refresh status. |
| Already checked in | Show previous accepted timestamp; do not create a second record. | Count as duplicate, not another attendee. |
| User not on roster | Explain that the user is not eligible for this session and show support/correction route. | Show no sensitive roster data. |
| No network | Queue only if a currently valid signed credential can be verified locally; show pending sync. | Show offline state and pending count. |
| Camera permission denied | Skip only if policy allows; otherwise show approved QR/PIN/facilitator fallback. | N/A unless kiosk supports assisted path. |
| Camera detects no face | Allow retry; never identify/label the person; show fallback after configured retries. | N/A. |
| Sync rejected | Preserve event locally with a clear reason and correction action. | Show rejected/pending-review count. |
| Kiosk revoked | Stop session creation and guide operator to contact admin. | Admin sees revoked state/audit event. |

## 16. Acceptance criteria

### Attend app

- A valid user can scan a current session QR and receive an accepted check-in result.
- A second scan for the same check-in displays the original result and does not create a new record.
- A signed QR expiring after 30 seconds is rejected after expiry.
- An event queued offline syncs once connectivity returns and yields one final backend result.
- A failed/denied camera permission has a documented alternate path.
- No network payload, local database record, log, or crash report contains a camera image, face landmark, face template, or face-matching result.
- A user can view their own attendance history and submit a correction request.

### Kiosk app

- An approved kiosk can start an assigned session and rotate its credential without exposing attendee information.
- An unapproved/revoked kiosk cannot start a session.
- Live counts reconcile with the backend after reconnecting.
- The device health view reports its last successful server contact and queue state.

### Backend/admin

- API rejects a client-provided `attendeeId` and always uses the authenticated user identity.
- API verifies signature, expiry, session scope, roster eligibility, and idempotency before accepting an event.
- Every correction includes the actor, reason, time, and before/after values.
- Exports and admin queries honor organization/branch/role scopes.

## 18. Test plan

### Automated tests

- Unit tests for QR signature parsing, expiry, policy gates, idempotency, state transitions, local queue encryption, and retry/backoff.
- API contract tests for every result state, cross-organization attempts, roster changes, replay attempts, revoked kiosks, and correction audit trail.
- Widget tests for all result/error/fallback states.
- Integration tests that switch airplane mode on/off during scan, submit, retry, and app restart.
- Security tests confirming no face image/landmark/template makes it into DTOs, device database, logs, analytics, or crash reporting.

### Manual pilot tests

- Busy entry/classroom: 30+ attendees scanning within a five-minute window.
- Low-light and older Android device QR/camera behavior.
- Clock changed forward/backward on attendee device.
- Session ends while a user is scanning.
- Kiosk restart mid-session.
- Admin revokes kiosk during an active session.
- Permission denied, no camera hardware, localization, screen reader, and large-font flows.

## 19. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| QR sharing or screenshots | Rotating, signed, short-lived credentials; session/roster validation; optional time/location checks; audit patterns. |
| Poor connectivity | Offline queue, clear provisional state, idempotent retries, and manual facilitator fallback. |
| False rejection / fairness issue | Never use camera as an identity system; give a non-camera fallback and correction path. |
| Duplicate records | UUID idempotency, server-led state machine, retry-by-query after timeouts. |
| Kiosk tampering | Managed-device enrollment, scoped device token, heartbeat, remote revoke, and minimal local data. |
| Scope creep into biometric identity | API/data schema prohibition, code review checklist, automated scan for prohibited image/template fields. |
| Privacy/regulatory concern | Documented purpose, opt-in/alternate path for optional checks, retention settings, access logs, and organization legal review. |

## 20. Open decisions for the product owner

These decisions are needed before implementation begins:

1. Is the first pilot for **students**, **employees**, or both? The roster and correction rules differ.
2. Which attendance types are required in MVP: check-in only, check-in/check-out, breaks, or all three?
3. Should offline attendance be enabled for all sites or only managed kiosk sites?
4. Which fallback is approved for users without camera permission/device access: manual short code, PIN, facilitator confirmation, or all three?
5. Are location verification and local camera-presence checks wanted at pilot launch, or deferred until after the QR/offline flow is stable?
6. What are the organization-specific retention periods for attendance records, audit records, pending local records, and correction attachments?
7. Does the existing TTStaffPro backend own the new session APIs, or will a dedicated attendance service be introduced?
