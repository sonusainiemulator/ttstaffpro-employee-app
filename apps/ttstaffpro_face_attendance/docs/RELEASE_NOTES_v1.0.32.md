### 🚀 What's New in v1.0.32 (Build 36)

#### 🗣️ Text-To-Speech (TTS) Voice Engine
- Multi-lingual audio greeting feedback on punch in/out (Hindi, English, Mute).
- Dynamic greeting phrases: *"Shubh Prabhat Amit Kumar, aapki haziri darj ho gayi hai"*.
- Quick language switcher directly accessible from Home screen.

#### 📴 Offline Attendance Queue & Auto-Sync
- Local encrypted disk queue for zero-data-loss punch recording in offline/poor network environments.
- Real-time connectivity listener and automatic background sync engine with exponential retry backoff.
- Live offline badge indicator with "Sync Now" on Home & Scan dashboards.

#### 📱 Tablet & Landscape Split Dashboard
- Responsive layout supporting Counter Stand Tablets & Landscape docks alongside Wall Mount Portrait terminals.
- Left-side live digital clock, time, attendance stats, and offline sync counter.
- Right-side real-time ML camera viewfinder.

#### 👁️ Multi-Frame Liveness & Anti-Spoofing
- Blink transition detection and head pose stability tracking over consecutive frames to block 2D photos and screen replays.

#### 🔢 Manual PIN / Employee Code Fallback
- Emergency PIN punch modal for masked or unrecognized employees.
